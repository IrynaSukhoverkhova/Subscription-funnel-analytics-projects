# 📊 Subscription Funnel, Recurring Revenue & Unit Economics

A comprehensive performance review and analytical report of a subscription mobile application ($N = 5,000$ users, Feb 2024 – Aug 2025 across 6 countries: US, UK, DE, BR, MX, PL).

---

## 🚀 Key Business Findings
* **Total Revenue:** $343,607 generated over the analyzed period.
* **Conversion Optimization:** Identified critical conversion-loss zones between trial start and first payment, particularly in Latin America (Brazil and Mexico).
* **The US Market Paradox:** The US demonstrated the highest profitability and resilience with an LTV/CAC ratio of **2.13x**, generating nearly half of the total company revenue ($125,201).
* **Channel Efficiency:** High-volume channels (Google Ads, TikTok Ads) showed lower long-term user quality, whereas Influencer campaigns and Apple Search Ads (ASA) delivered the highest ROMI and retention.
